Privacy policy

Our Compass, by The Spectrum Tech Engine. Last updated 17 October 2026.

The short version

Everything you put into Our Compass stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no sign-up and no adverts. We never see your child's name, your diary, check-ins, notes or "All about me" passport.

What is saved, and where

  • On your phone only: each child's name or nickname, picture, age group, settings, check-ins, social battery, star chart, stories, the diary of tricky and good moments, the calm plan, the "All about me" passport and your child's private notes.
  • It's saved in the browser's storage on your phone (or inside the Android app). The website and the Android app keep separate copies.
  • If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, it's deleted. Use Save backup in Grown-ups → Settings to keep a copy.

When you choose to share

  • Backup file: a file saved to your phone, or sent wherever you choose (for example to another parent or carer). It contains everything, including your child's private notes, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Summary and "Send as text": these go only where you send them, such as an email or message to a doctor or school.
  • Passport QR code: the passport is packed inside the code itself, after a "#" in the link. Browsers never send that part to a website, so we don't receive it. Anyone who has the code or link can read the passport, so only share it with people you trust.

The few things that use the internet

  • Opening the app: the app's pages are loaded from our website (hosted by GitHub Pages), like any website.
  • Anonymous usage counts: to see which parts are useful, the app counts things like "a check-in happened" or "a summary was printed". These counts are anonymous. They never include names, words you typed, which colour was picked, or anything from the diary.
  • Feedback: if you tap Send feedback, the message you type is sent to us. Please don't include your child's name.
  • Easy-read font: if switched on, the font is downloaded from Google Fonts.
  • QR code maker: the first time you make a QR code, a small code library is downloaded from jsDelivr. Your passport is made into a QR code on your phone, not online.

Children

Our Compass is made for parents and carers to use with their children. Children's information stays on the family's own phone and is never collected by us.

Not medical advice

Our Compass supports, but doesn't replace, a speech and language therapist, occupational therapist, psychologist, GP or CAMHS. Patterns it shows are things that happened together, not proof of what causes what.
