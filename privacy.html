Privacy policy

Our Compass, by The Spectrum Tech Engine. Last updated 19 October 2026.

The short version

There are no accounts, no sign-up and no adverts. Your diary stays on your phone. If you link your child's own phone or tablet, only your family's linked devices can read what they share: it's scrambled (encrypted) on the phone before it leaves, and the key never leaves your family's devices. We never see your child's name, your diary, check-ins, notes or "All about me" passport.

What is saved, and where

  • On the phone it was written on, and never shared: the diary of tricky and good moments, patterns, the summary, the professional's name, and your child's private notes.
  • On your phone, and on your child's linked device if you link one: each child's name or nickname, picture, age group, settings, check-ins, social battery, wins, star chart, stories, your own moments and hidden rules, calm ideas, the calm plan, the "All about me" passport, and the child's drawings.
  • It's saved in the browser's storage on your phone (or inside the Android app). The website and the Android app keep separate copies.
  • If you clear your browser data or uninstall the app, it's deleted. Use Save backup in Parent → Settings to keep a copy.

Linking your child's own device

  • It's your choice. Nothing is linked unless your child's device shows its linking code and you scan it or type it in on your phone.
  • Scrambled before it leaves the phone. When you link, your phone makes a random secret key and passes it to your child's device scrambled with their one-time code, so we can't read it. Everything shared is scrambled with that key (AES-256, the same kind of encryption banks use) on the phone, before it's sent.
  • Where it waits. The scrambled copy is kept on Google Firebase (our own Firebase project) so the other device can pick it up, even when the two aren't online at the same time. Without your family's key it's unreadable, to us, to Google, and to anyone else. Each box is named from the key, so nobody can find it or list it.
  • The one-time code is only used once. The scrambled key waits on the server for 15 minutes at most, and is deleted as soon as the child's device picks it up.
  • What goes where. From your phone to your child's device: their settings, tools, theme, star chart, Coming up stories, your own moments and rules, "All about me", calm ideas, the calm plan if you make one, timers you start for them, and good moments you choose to show in "My wins". From your child's device to you: check-ins, social battery, wins they add, and their drawings, plus "All about me" or their picture if they change them.
  • Never shared: your diary, patterns and summary, and your child's private notes.
  • Unlinking (Parent → Devices) deletes the scrambled copies from the server. Each device keeps what it already has.
  • Delete everything (Parent → Settings) deletes the scrambled copies from the server and everything on the parent's phone, so you can start over.

When you choose to share

  • Backup file: a file saved to your phone, or sent wherever you choose (for example to another parent or carer). It contains everything, including your child's private notes, so keep it somewhere safe.
  • Summary and "Send as text": these go only where you send them, such as an email or message to a doctor or school.
  • Passport QR code: the passport is packed inside the code itself, after a "#" in the link. Browsers never send that part to a website, so we don't receive it. Anyone who has the code or link can read the passport, so only share it with people you trust.

The few things that use the internet

  • Opening the app: the app's pages are loaded from our website (hosted by GitHub Pages), like any website.
  • Linked devices: only if you link one, as explained above. Only scrambled data is sent.
  • Anonymous usage counts: to see which parts are useful, the app counts things like "a check-in happened" or "a summary was printed". These counts are anonymous. They never include names, words you typed, which colour was picked, or anything from the diary.
  • Feedback: if you tap Send feedback, the message you type is sent to us. Please don't include your child's name.
  • Easy-read font: if switched on, the font is downloaded from Google Fonts.
  • QR code maker and scanner: the first time you make or scan a QR code, a small code library may be downloaded from jsDelivr. QR codes are made and read on the phone, not online. The camera is only used while the scanner is open, and the picture never leaves the phone.

Children

Our Compass is made for parents and carers to use with their children. Children's information stays on the family's own devices. If devices are linked, it's scrambled before it leaves the phone, and we can't read it.

Not medical advice

Our Compass supports, but doesn't replace, a speech and language therapist, occupational therapist, psychologist, GP or CAMHS. Patterns it shows are things that happened together, not proof of what causes what.
